About stretch film supply for Dada Nagar

The industrial lanes of Dada Nagar are full of workshops and small factories where space is tight and packing is practical rather than elaborate. Stretch film fits well here: it keeps cartons and bundles together, protects them from dust and handling marks, and needs no special equipment when hand-grade rolls are used.

Hand-grade film for compact units

Smaller units often wrap a few pallets or a few dozen parcels at a time. Hand-grade stretch film, applied with a simple hand dispenser or by walking around the load, is the usual choice. As your volumes grow, moving to machine-grade film and a wrapping machine can save time per pallet. We can discuss both options when you enquire.

Mention whether you wrap pallets, loose cartons or odd-shaped items. Odd shapes often need a different roll width than standard pallet loads.
