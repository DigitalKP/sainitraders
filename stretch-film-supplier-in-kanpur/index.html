What to Check Before Finalising a Kanpur Stretch Film Rate

Many Kanpur buyers compare only the per-roll price and end up paying more per wrapped pallet. Before confirming any quotation, check four numbers side by side:

  • Paper core weight vs. net LLDPE weight: A cheap 3 kg gross roll with a 900 g core gives far less usable film than an honest roll with a light, standard core.
  • True micron consistency: Under-gauge film snaps mid-wrap, forcing workers to double the layers and burn extra rolls every week.
  • Roll width matched to load height: Standard wide rolls speed up pallet wrapping, while narrow utility rolls reduce overhang waste on small bundles.
  • Local replacement accountability: Sourcing directly inside Kanpur lets your storekeeper verify weight on delivery and resolve batch issues immediately.
