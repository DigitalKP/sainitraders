Frequently Asked Questions

What is stretch film used for in industrial packaging?

LLDPE stretch film wraps tightly around stacked cartons, pallets, drums, and loose bundles to lock loads together and shield goods from dust, scuffs, and transit moisture.

What is the difference between hand-grade and machine-grade stretch film?

Hand-grade rolls are lighter and built for manual wrapping or hand dispensers. Machine-grade rolls are wound for semi-automatic or automatic pallet wrappers requiring steady pre-stretch tension and higher output.

How do I check I am getting real net film weight and not a heavy paper core?

Always ask for declared gross weight, paper core weight, and net film weight separately. SAINI TRADERS declares both weights upfront and allows weight verification at delivery.

Yes. Shopkeepers, movers, and small units can order single rolls, while factories and godowns receive direct wholesale rates on carton and recurring monthly orders.
