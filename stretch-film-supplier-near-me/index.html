Frequently Asked Questions

What is stretch film used for?

It is a stretchable plastic film wrapped around goods or pallets to hold them together and protect them from dust and handling during storage and transport.

What is the difference between hand-grade and machine-grade stretch film?

Hand-grade rolls are meant to be applied manually. Machine-grade rolls are designed for pallet wrapping machines and consistent, repeatable wrapping.

Do you supply single rolls as well as bulk?
