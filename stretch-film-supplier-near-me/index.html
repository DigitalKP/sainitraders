Types of Stretch Film Available

Hand Grade Stretch Film

Lightweight manual rolls applied by hand or dispenser. Ideal for shops, courier bundling, furniture protection, and units wrapping low-to-moderate pallet volumes without investing in machinery.

Machine Grade Stretch Film

High-elongation rolls engineered for semi-automatic and automatic pallet wrappers. Delivers consistent holding force, lower film consumption per pallet, and faster line speed for factories and godowns.

Transparent Stretch Film

High-clarity clear film that keeps carton shipping marks, GST invoices, and warehouse barcodes instantly scannable during storage, stock audits, and transit handovers.

Black Stretch Film

Opaque UV-shielding film that conceals high-value goods against pilferage, blocks sunlight on light-sensitive stock, and lets warehouses colour-code priority consignments.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is stretch film used for?

It is a stretchable LLDPE plastic film wrapped around goods or pallets to hold them together and protect them from dust and handling during storage and transport.

What is the difference between hand-grade and machine-grade stretch film?

Hand-grade rolls are meant to be applied manually. Machine-grade rolls are designed for pallet wrapping machines and consistent, repeatable wrapping.
